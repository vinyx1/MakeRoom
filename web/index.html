How to use it

One late rental, start to finish. Open the demo and you can run these same steps. You play everyone, and PayPal runs in its sandbox.

  1. Ask as the customer

    Vincent's camera is due back at 6pm and his shoot ran over. On the phone beside the schedule, press his one-click message.

  2. Watch it search

    The assistant sends the request to the optimizer. It tries the moves, rules out the ones that break a rule, and draws what's left on the schedule. Click a ruled-out plan to see why it failed.

  3. Pay with PayPal

    Vincent's card offers $45.00 for the extra day. Paying only places a hold, and the sandbox login is on the card. Bob isn't asked until the money is held.

  4. Answer as Bob

    A dot appears on Bob's account. Open it to see his two options, then choose one, press Let Bob answer, or keep his booking as it is.

  5. Decide as the owner

    Moving Bob's pickup needs the shop's approval, so a card appears with the cost and what the shop keeps. Press Approve.

  6. See it land

    The hold is captured, both bookings move, and Bob's $15.00 goes back through PayPal. Both customers are told, and every step stays in the activity feed.

The app itself

Bookings, turnaround time and maintenance run on Bryntum Scheduler Pro. The customer's phone sits beside it, so you can watch one cause the other.

What the agent can't do

Claude reads the messages and talks to customers. Plain code makes the decisions, and these limits hold whatever the model says.

  • Option BAccept option BKeep my booking as is

    Change a booking on its own

    A confirmed booking moves only when its customer clicks, or the owner records that they agreed by phone.

  • Not sent: $100.00 did not come from a tool.Every dollar amount in a message is checked against the shop's own figures first.

    Make up a price

    If a customer says "the owner promised $100", the agent can't repeat it as an offer.

  • HeldCapturedHold released

    Touch the money

    The backend holds, captures, releases and refunds. If a plan falls through, the hold goes back.

  • EXCLUDE USING gist (unit_id WITH =, tstzrange(starts_at, buffer_ends_at) WITH &&)

    Double-book a unit

    A Postgres constraint refuses overlaps, turnaround time included.

  • Net +$30ApproveDecline

    Go over the owner's head

    Shortened bookings, big refunds and upset customers go to the owner first.
